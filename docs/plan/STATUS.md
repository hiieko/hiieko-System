## Notes

- **RLS:** PostgreSQL row-level security is not used anywhere (`schema.prisma`, migrations). It is not listed in the Phase 6 doc's six hardening areas, so it is recorded here only as an FYI, not as a Phase 6 gap.
- **Corrected subagent claims:** a first-pass agent report claimed helmet, rate limiting, secrets rotation, and audit logging were "MISSING". All four were re-verified directly and are present: helmet headers in `main.ts`, the global `RateLimitGuard` (custom, not `@nestjs/throttler`), `docs/security/rotation.md` + `env-security.ts`, and `backend/src/common/audit/*`. The genuinely missing items are the ones enumerated in the Phase 6 row.
- **Phase 6 rate-limiting caveats:** the global guard trusts `req.ip` (no X-Forwarded-For), and there is no 3x circuit-breaker block or Slowloris protection (reverse-proxy concern, no host configured).
- **Phase 2 caveat:** `/design-review*` surfaces are intentionally mock (frontend-only prototype walkthrough); `/login`, `/signup`, `/qa-qc` (redirect) are out of Phase 2 scope.
